# Regenerative Agriculture Approach

AgriN Connect operationalises a small set of clear, field-level practices in its advisory responses.

| Practice | When the prototype recommends it | Intended outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Living roots / legume cover crop | All field briefs | Support soil cover and nitrogen cycling |
| Reduced tillage | All field briefs | Retain moisture and protect soil structure |
| Residue mulch | Low water availability | Reduce evaporation and suppress weeds |
| Lower field traffic after rain | Clay soil | Reduce compaction and preserve drainage |
| Pulses and intercrops | Suitable crop/season combinations | Increase diversity and resilience |

These are prototype recommendations, not prescriptive agronomy. Production deployment requires local validation by extension partners, accounting for crop, geography, inputs, and farmer priorities.
