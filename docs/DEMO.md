# Submission demo script

1. Open the React app and introduce **AgriN Connect** as a farmer-first, interoperable intelligence network.
2. Go to **Crops**. Set `Loamy`, `Kharif`, and `Medium` water, then generate recommendations. Point out crop diversity and the regenerative-fit badge.
3. Open **Field Intel** and generate the field brief. Show the NDVI, soil health, forecast risk, and the plain-language regenerative actions.
4. Highlight the **BRICS Cooperation by Design** panel: consent, local sovereignty, open contract, and model provenance.
5. Open **Disease** and upload a leaf image to demonstrate the image-inference workflow and safe referral wording.
6. Close with the FastAPI `/cooperation/model-card` endpoint, which makes the model inputs, outputs, and governance reusable by partner deployments.

## Judge-facing talking points

- The data layer is intentionally offline-ready for demonstration reliability and low-connectivity contexts.
- The interface separates observations from recommendations, so a farmer can understand *why* a suggestion appears.
- The same contract can plug into national satellite, soil, and meteorological providers.
- Cooperation is privacy-preserving: share models and aggregated indicators, not farmers' precise locations by default.
